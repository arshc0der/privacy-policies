Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Flowra mobile application (the “App”) handles information. The App is created and operated by Arsh (“we”, “us”, “the Developer”). By using the App you agree to this policy.

In short: Flowra does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal information. Everything you create in the App stays in the App’s private storage on your own device.

Information stored on your device

To work, the App saves the following on your phone only. We never receive a copy.

  • Profile: the name you enter, an optional profile photo and your personal quote.
  • Focus and breathing sessions: dates, durations, categories and linked tasks.
  • Tasks: titles, notes, priority, due dates, reminder settings and completion status.
  • Mood check-ins and journal notes: the mood you choose, intensity, feelings, what shaped your day, worries and any note you write.
  • Presets and settings: your custom focus presets, timer lengths, daily goal, theme, accent colour and haptics choice.
  • Timer state: the status and end time of a running timer, so it can keep working while the App is closed.

This information is held in the App’s local storage, which is protected by your device’s app sandbox and screen lock. It is not uploaded anywhere.

Information we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or account details.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, messages, call logs or files from your device.
  • No device identifiers, advertising IDs, usage analytics or crash reports.
  • No payment information. The App is free and has no purchases.

How your information is used

Your on-device information is used only to run the features you see: showing your history and statistics, calculating streaks, levels and insights, and scheduling your own reminders. All of this happens locally on your phone. No automated decisions are made about you, and nothing is used for advertising or profiling.

Notifications and your lock screen

Reminders and timer alerts are created by the App on your device. They may show your task titles or timer status on your lock screen, depending on your phone’s notification settings. You can hide this in your system settings, or turn notifications off at any time.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo picker are used only when you choose to set a profile picture. The picture you select is resized and kept inside the App on your device. It is never uploaded. You can remove it at any time in Profile.

Sharing, export and import

If you tap Export my data or Share Flowra, your phone’s standard share sheet opens and you decide where the text goes, for example a notes app or a message. We do not see or receive it. To restore a backup, you paste your own exported text into the import box in the App. Please store backups somewhere private, because they contain your journal and task entries.

Third-party services

The App does not include advertising networks, analytics tools or tracking services, and it does not send your data to any third party. It is built with open-source software libraries that run entirely on your device to provide features such as local storage, notifications, vibration and the photo picker.

If you download Flowra from Google Play, Google processes that download under its own privacy policy. That is separate from this App and outside our control.

Data retention and deletion

Your information stays on your device until you remove it. You are in full control:

  • Delete a single mood check-in, task or preset inside the App.
  • Use Reset everything in Profile to erase all data stored by the App.
  • Uninstall the App, or clear its storage in your phone settings, to remove everything.

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. If your phone’s backup feature is turned on, your operating system provider may back up app data to your own account under its own policies.

Security

Keeping data only on your device is our main protection, since there is no server to breach. We recommend a screen lock on your phone, and careful handling of any exported backup. No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s privacy

Flowra is a general-audience wellbeing and productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, no data is gathered from children either. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, contact us and we will help.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA or India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act may give you rights to access, correct, delete or move your data, or to object to its use. Since we do not collect or hold your personal data, these rights are fulfilled directly in the App: you can view, edit, export and delete everything yourself. We do not sell or share personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes, for example if a new feature needs a new permission. The effective date at the top will change, and significant changes will be described in the App’s update notes on Google Play. If we ever add a feature that collects data, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent where required.

App permissions

What Flowra asks for, and why

Flowra asks only for what its features need. None of these permissions give us access to your data, because the App has no server to send it to.

NotificationsRecommended

Shows your timer controls (pause, resume, stop), task reminders and the alert when a session ends.

Alarms and remindersRecommended

Lets reminders and the end-of-session alert arrive at the exact time, even when the App is closed.

Background activityRecommended

Lets your timer keep running when you switch apps or close Flowra. You allow this in your phone’s battery settings. The App only opens that settings screen, and cannot read your other apps.

VibrationRecommended

Gentle haptic feedback, breathing cues and a vibration when a session ends. You can turn it off in Profile.

Run at startupRecommended

Lets scheduled reminders continue to work after you restart your phone.

CameraOptional

Used only if you choose to take a profile photo. The App works fully without it.

PhotosOptional

Used only when you pick an existing picture for your profile. You select the single image yourself.

You can change or withdraw any permission at any time in your phone’s settings. If you turn one off, the related feature stops working, but the rest of the App continues to work.
